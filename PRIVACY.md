## What this policy covers

This policy covers the CLAW Windows application and Launcher, the official CLAW website, and correspondence sent to the project’s legal, privacy and private security address. [CLAW website](https://claw-painting.pages.dev)

## Desktop application and Launcher

CLAW works offline without an account. Painting, saving, opening projects, and using the Launcher do not transmit artwork, diagnostics, or crash reports to the CLAW developer. Online updates are disabled in this beta. When you choose an official website or release notes link in CLAW, it opens in your default browser. Your browser, operating system, and software from other providers are governed by their own privacy settings and policies.

CLAW stores settings, recent project paths, working data and Recovery copies on your computer. Projects can contain embedded Reference images and recording settings. User-imported brush data is stored locally. If you use the Recording feature, temporary recording data and exported files are processed on your computer. Optional MP4 export uses a separately installed FFmpeg executable and passes recording frames to it on your computer. Projects, exports and images you choose to open may also be stored at paths you select. CLAW may save Recovery data until you restore, discard, or otherwise manage it. Uninstalling preserves your artwork and local CLAW data.

You can start CLAW with optional local diagnostics. They can include CLAW and Windows versions, GPU and rendering backend, display and input-device measurements, timing and error counts, and a panic source location. The report is stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CLAW\diagnostics.txt`. It is not sent automatically. Check any report before choosing to share it.

## Website and browser storage

The official site uses Cloudflare Pages. When you open a page or request an asset, Cloudflare can receive your IP address, request time and URL, and HTTP headers such as browser details or referrer. Cloudflare uses request data to deliver the site and can use it for service security and troubleshooting. Cloudflare Web Analytics is disabled for this project. CLAW serves its images and fonts from its own origin. Its site code sets no cookies and includes no analytics or tracking scripts. Cloudflare may perform browser and request security checks at its network edge. [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) · [Cloudflare Pages platform notes](https://developers.cloudflare.com/pages/platform/known-issues/)

When a public installer becomes available and you follow its download link, your browser connects to GitHub and its asset delivery service. GitHub can receive request, browser and IP data and, if you are signed in, information associated with your GitHub account. GitHub describes its own collection and use in its privacy statement. CLAW’s desktop app receives no download report. [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
